💾 Savefiles

Pick a save to work on. Everything autosaves in this browser: your imported stats and marked cards. Saves stay on this device; use Download JSON on the Stats page to move one to another device.

    📥 Import your stats
    How do I export my stats?
    1. In the game, open the Stats menu.
    2. Scroll down all the way to the bottom.
    3. Tap Export. Your stats are copied to the clipboard as JSON.
    4. Paste them into the box below (or save them to a .json file and pick it).

    Tip: unequip temporary buffs first if you want your base stats. The export reflects what is active at that moment.

    🃏 Cards

    The stats export doesn't include your cards, so mark them here. Pick a level, then click cards to set them (click again to remove). Shift+click fills every card between your last click and this one. On a phone, turn on Range, tap the first card, then the last.

    💣 Bomb setup

    Each bomb's damage multiplier: the “×” number shown next to the bomb in game (e.g. 98×). The export only includes the shared part, so enter the real values here. Empty fields use the export's shared multiplier. Saved with this save.

    Values are editable. Changes are saved in this browser only.

    Not in the export

    The export always contains the same stats, with 0 for anything you haven't unlocked yet. These systems are on the wiki but have no stats in the export (as of v2.2.30). If a future game update adds them, they appear in their section or under Other.
